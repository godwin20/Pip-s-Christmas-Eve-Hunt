Delete your data

Last updated: 30 October 2026

You can delete everything Pip's Christmas Eve Hunt keeps about you: straight away in the game, or by asking us by email. You don't need to open or reinstall the game to ask.

🗑️ In the game

  1. Open Pip's Christmas Eve Hunt on the phone, tablet or computer you play on (the Android app, or the web game).
  2. On the title screen, tap 🔒 Privacy (at the bottom, next to "Reset progress"). It is also under Leaderboard, in the "Play on any device" card.
  3. Tap Delete my saved data, then Yes, delete it.
  4. Signed in with Google? The game may ask you to confirm once more: tap Confirm with Google to finish deleting and pick the same Google account.

In the game it happens straight away.

On Android, the app and Chrome share the game's saved data, so you can also do this in Chrome at godwin20.github.io/Pip-s-Christmas-Eve-Hunt.

✉️ By email

Email keithstack1@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". If you signed in with Google, send it from that Google account's email address (or tell us which address it was), so we can find the account and know the request is yours.

If you email us, we delete it within 30 days and tell you when it's done.

Only ever played as a guest? A guest's save has no name or email on it, so an email can't tell us which one is yours. Please use the button in the game on that device instead.

What is deleted

Deleting removes the cloud save (progress, snow name and scores), the leaderboard entry, any feedback you sent and a Google sign-in, with its email address, name and profile photo.

Your Google account itself stays as it is: only its link to this game goes.

On the device, the game starts fresh with a new snow name. Only the sound settings stay.

What is kept for a while, and why

Google keeps its own security logs, which include IP addresses, for a few weeks, and deleted data leaves Google's backups within about 180 days.

Google keeps these to protect its services from abuse and to recover from faults. They are not used to play the game or to contact you, and Google deletes them on its own schedule.

A guest's sign-in holds only the random player ID. Firebase may keep that ID, but nothing is stored under it any more.

Firebase only deletes a sign-in that signed in during the last few minutes. A guest can't sign in again to confirm, so the game signs out of it instead. On its own the ID can't be linked to you. Nothing else is kept.

How long it takes

In the game it happens straight away. If you email us, we delete it within 30 days and tell you when it's done.

If you don't delete anything

How long: until you delete it. The game's maker may also clear away, by hand, saves, leaderboard entries and sign-ins that haven't been used for 2 years.
